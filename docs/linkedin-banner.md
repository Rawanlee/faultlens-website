## Design
- **Background:** Ink #0F1720 with a faint 24–36 px grid (inspection / blueprint feel) and a soft amber glow on the right.
- **Logo:** the Faultlens mark — an amber lens with a white fault line inside, an amber handle and a green "fixed" dot — plus the wordmark "fault**lens**" (lens in Signal Amber).
- **Message:** "Show us what's broken." (Paper #F6F4EF) / "We'll find who can fix it." (Signal Amber #FFB224).
- **Supporting line:** AI fault diagnosis · Verified technicians · From $1/month · faultlens.space.
- **Product UI:** a Fault brief card (item, likely cause, urgency, trade) ending in "3 verified technicians matched" with a Fix Green #19A974 check.

## Safe zones
- Company banner: the page logo overlaps the bottom-left on desktop and the centre on mobile, so text starts at x = 268 px and nothing important sits in the left 240 px.
- 1584 × 396: the profile photo covers roughly the left 470 px of the lower half, so text starts at x = 500 px.
- Keep a check on mobile after uploading: LinkedIn crops the sides of the banner on small screens.

## Fonts
SVGs use Helvetica Neue / Arial so they render anywhere; the website uses Space Grotesk (headings) and Inter (body).
